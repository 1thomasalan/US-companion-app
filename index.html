First question: can't or won't?

Class companion for a Marine Corps NAF supervisor at MCCS Okinawa.

Wed Nov 4 to Thu Nov 5, 2026 · 12 sessions

NAF is different. Substantial-evidence standard for misconduct and performance. A Letter of Requirement comes before a PIP. Appeals go to the Head of the Local NAFI, then HQMC (MR); no MSPB.

Misconduct is won't; performance is can't.
